Diagnosis

The monorepo has a solid semantic core for its stage (--ink, --bg, --muted in design-system/), but the app and landing still have ~43% literal values and three sources of truth that have already diverged.

The main opportunity is not “more tokens”, but consolidating a single source (remove the copy in service-web), unify accents (--color-primary vs #2383e2) and split AppChrome.tsx (888 LOC).

Estimated effort: 0.4–0.6 person-months over 4–6 weeks, compatible with parallel product development.

What not to touch now

Migrate the entire app to Tailwind v4
Tailwind is in package.json but without @apply or classes in src. The current stack (CSS Modules + DS) works and fits Tauri. Migrating now would duplicate paradigms.
When to reconsider: if you hire a dev who only works utility-first, or you need automated design tokens → Tailwind theme sync.
Hybrid 3-level architecture (primitive → semantic → component)
With 11 global tokens and 1 brand, adding blue-500 + color-text-primary + button-bg is overhead without multi-product or active dark mode.
When to reconsider: dark mode on the roadmap, white-label, or a second product with a distinct identity.
Storybook / formal component documentation
At pre-seed with 1 designer-owner, the cost of maintaining Storybook outweighs the benefit. The DS is CSS-first, not a React library.
When to reconsider: third frontend dev or first external UI contractor.
Massive section refactor (DealFinancePanel 819 LOC)
These are product screens, not DS primitives. Splitting AppChrome has more ROI than stable business sections.
When to reconsider: when DealFinancePanel changes every sprint or breaks tests.
