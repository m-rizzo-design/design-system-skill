## Do not do now

### Migrate the entire app to Tailwind v4

**Why not:** Tailwind is in package.json but unused; CSS Modules + DS fits Tauri. Migrating now duplicates paradigms.

**Reconsider when:** you hire a utility-first-only dev, or need automated tokens → Tailwind theme sync.

### Hybrid 3-level architecture (primitive → semantic → component)

**Why not:** 11 global tokens and 1 brand — primitive scales are overhead without multi-product or active dark mode.

**Reconsider when:** dark mode on roadmap, white-label, or second product with distinct identity.

### Storybook / formal component documentation

**Why not:** At pre-seed with 1 designer-owner, Storybook cost outweighs benefit. DS is CSS-first.

**Reconsider when:** third frontend dev or first external UI contractor.

### Massive section refactor (DealFinancePanel 819 LOC)

**Why not:** Product screens, not DS primitives. Splitting AppChrome has more ROI.

**Reconsider when:** DealFinancePanel changes every sprint or breaks tests.
